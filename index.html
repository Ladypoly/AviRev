Every generation of the wardrobe. Inspect its materials, geometry and Unity availability.

Textured views show the authored Blender materials. Studio lighting differs from Unity. Diagnostic comparison colors distinguish garment surfaces; clay and wire views expose geometry. Outfit studies include three static body profiles.

Unity labels: green means currently selectable, amber means imported or used as a dependency, red means source only. Labels are matched to the exported Unity catalogs, not guessed from Blender collection names.

Triangle counts: source mesh triangles are calculated from Blender mesh topology. Evaluated render triangles, when available, include the modifiers and body cuts used for these renders. Outfit source totals include the reference body; garment-only counts are shown separately in the details.

Issue labels: confirmed checks and documented follow-ups have separate statuses. An item without a listed issue has not automatically passed a full review.
